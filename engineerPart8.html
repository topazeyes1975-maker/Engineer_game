Electronics Lab: Basic LED Circuit & Resistors

The Rule (Ohm's Law): V = I x R (Voltage = Current x Resistance).
To find the safe resistor (R), subtract the LED voltage drop from the source, then divide by desired current: R = (V_source - V_LED) / Current.
Example: (5V - 2V) / 0.014A is approximately 0.214 = 214 ohms (Standard close value: ??? Ω).
